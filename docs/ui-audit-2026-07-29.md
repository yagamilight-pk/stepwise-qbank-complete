# Stepwise UI audit

Audit date: July 29, 2026

Evidence reviewed:

- desktop, tablet, and mobile captures from `artifacts/visual-review`
- landing, guided trial, auth, checkout, support, policy, learner, session, admin, and partner routes
- interaction checks for the highlighter, question sessions, responsive tools, security headers, and overflow

## Fixed in this pass

- The landing audit card became **Readiness without guesswork.** with four qualitative signals and a direct due-flashcards action.
- The mobile support header now preserves both primary actions without clipping.
- The mobile admin organ-system chart now places its legend below the chart with full labels and values.
- The mobile auth switch and legal copy have more breathing room.
- Anonymous/demo routes no longer generate misleading failed cloud-sync requests.
- The partner surface no longer asks for a local/shared password. It clearly explains the Appwrite role and provisioning requirements.
- Commercial pages show a truthful disabled state while payment and merchant requirements are incomplete.
- The highlighter acts only on selected question-stem text and explains what to do when no text is selected.

## Recommended next UI directions

### 1. A daily command center

**What it improves:** The mobile learner overview is coherent but very long, especially for a new learner with no data.

**Direction:** Keep the readiness signal, current streak, and today's plan above the fold. Collapse secondary metrics into “Progress”, “Mastery”, and “Insights” disclosures until the learner has enough data for them to be useful.

**Success measure:** A learner can identify and begin the highest-value study action in under ten seconds without scrolling past empty analytics.

### 2. Evidence-first analytics

**What it improves:** Empty and low-sample charts are honest, but several screens still devote large surfaces to zero-value graphics.

**Direction:** Replace empty charts with a compact evidence state that says how many completed questions are needed, what the future metric means, and one button that creates the required evidence.

**Success measure:** No score, percentile, readiness estimate, or calibration label appears without sample size, method, and a next action.

### 3. Operations you can trust

**What it improves:** The admin and partner interfaces look mature, but their production workflows remain intentionally limited.

**Direction:** Back the existing surfaces with Appwrite roles, server-side records, immutable audit events, review assignments, entitlement history, partner attribution, commissions, and payout approvals. Keep demo data visually distinct from production data.

**Success measure:** Every visible operational action has persisted state, an accountable actor, a timestamp, an authorization check, and a recoverable audit trail.

## Follow-up polish

- Add compact field-level password guidance and a Caps Lock warning without increasing form height.
- Add an “email sent / resend available in…” state to verification and recovery pages once custom SMTP is live.
- Add skeletons only to data-backed routes that can actually wait on a server response; avoid decorative loading.
- Add manual WCAG checks for keyboard focus order, screen-reader announcements, 200% zoom, and 400% reflow before making a conformance claim.

## Current visual conclusion

No unresolved P0 or P1 visual defects were found in the captured primary journeys. The shipped responsive fixes address the material clipping and clarity problems found during this pass. The daily command center, evidence-first analytics, and production operations directions are product upgrades, not visual launch regressions.
